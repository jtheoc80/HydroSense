# Estimates and invoices plumbing compliance follow-up

Status: Separate compliance follow-up; out of scope for PR #10.

## Objective

Audit HydroSense estimates, proposals, invoices, receipts, installation records, quote emails, and related transactional templates for any Texas plumbing disclosures required by current law, regulation, licensing guidance, contract terms, or insurer documentation requirements.

## Review scope

- Confirm which documents require the Responsible Master Plumber identifier and its exact official format.
- Determine whether the plumbing company, license holder, certificate-of-insurance status, or other regulatory details must appear on each document type.
- Verify required placement, prominence, timing, and retention rules.
- Compare the findings with every current estimate, invoice, receipt, proposal, installation-record, email, SMS, and downloadable document template.
- Define tests that prevent required transaction-level disclosures from being removed or shown on the wrong document.
- Obtain qualified Texas plumbing compliance or legal review before changing production transactional language.

## Guardrails

- Do not expose private fulfillment details on normal lead-generation pages or in public structured data.
- Do not treat this follow-up as authorization to change transactional workflows in PR #10.
- Use the governed internal evidence record when fuller regulatory details are required.
- Keep HydroSense the customer-facing service and lead destination.
