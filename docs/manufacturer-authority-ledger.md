| Manufacturer | Authority tier | Authorization status | Public corroboration | Owner action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FloLogic | Tier 1 | AUTHORIZED — OWNER VERIFIED | NOT PUBLICLY CORROBORATED | Obtain/store exact public manufacturer designation and public profile URL, if available. |
| Phyn | Tier 1 | AUTHORIZED — OWNER VERIFIED | NOT PUBLICLY CORROBORATED | Obtain/store exact public manufacturer designation and public profile URL, if available. |
